# SmallHub Biz

SmallHub Biz is a student-focused online marketplace designed to connect students with student-owned businesses within the university community.

The platform gives student entrepreneurs a place to showcase their businesses, products, and services, while making it easier for students to discover and support businesses around them.

---

## About the Project

Many students run small businesses while studying, but finding customers and gaining visibility can be difficult.

SmallHub Biz aims to create a dedicated digital marketplace where student entrepreneurs can promote what they offer and where other students can easily discover businesses, products, and services within their university community.

The platform is designed around three main goals:

* Help student businesses gain visibility
* Make it easier for students to discover and support student entrepreneurs
* Encourage entrepreneurship within the university community

## Vision

The long-term vision of SmallHub Biz is to become a digital ecosystem for student entrepreneurship.

The platform can grow beyond simply showcasing businesses and eventually provide students with tools to:

* Create and manage business profiles
* List products and services
* Reach customers on campus
* Communicate with customers
* Manage orders
* Track business activity
* Promote their businesses
* Build their customer base

---

## Future Development

Planned development areas include:

### Business Profiles

Dedicated profiles where student entrepreneurs can provide information about their business, products, services, operating hours, location, and contact details.

### Product Listings

Businesses will be able to showcase individual products and services with descriptions, images, prices, and availability.

### Search & Filtering

Students will be able to search for businesses and filter them by category, location, price, and other criteria.

### Authentication

Users will eventually be able to create accounts and securely access platform features.

### Business Registration

Student entrepreneurs will be able to register their businesses directly through the platform.

### Location-Based Discovery

Users will be able to discover businesses based on their location and distance from them.

### Reviews & Ratings

Customers may eventually be able to review businesses and products, helping build trust within the marketplace.
