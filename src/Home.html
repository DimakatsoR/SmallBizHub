ABOUT SMALLHUB BIZ

Connecting Students. Growing Businesses.

SmallHub Biz is an online platform built to connect students with student-owned businesses within the university community and beyond. It gives student entrepreneurs a place to showcase their businesses, products, and services, while making it easier for fellow students to discover and support them.

Our goal is to create a thriving student marketplace where entrepreneurship is encouraged, businesses gain visibility, and students have the opportunity to support and grow alongside fellow student entrepreneurs.
